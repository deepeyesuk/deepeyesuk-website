Support & Help Centre

Help with charging estimates, saved setups and troubleshooting for the Charge Check iPhone app.

Check your charging setup

Charge Check estimates how quickly your iPhone battery is charging and helps you compare chargers and cables.

  1. Connect your charger. Open Charge Check and keep it in the foreground while measuring. For a useful comparison, start below 80% battery and avoid demanding apps.
  2. Wait for a reading. The app waits for the next 1% increase, then times the following 1%. This can take several minutes. Early readings are preliminary; allow more readings for a steadier estimate.
  3. Save a setup. When a wattage reading appears, tap + Label this Setup, enter a name and tap Save. Open Setups to rename or delete saved entries.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the wattage printed on my charger?

No. Charge Check estimates power entering the battery from battery-percentage changes and an estimated battery capacity. It does not directly measure the charger’s output or identify its hardware. Temperature, battery condition, device use and charging losses affect results. A saved setup match means a similar estimated rate, not confirmed charger identification.

Why does the app keep saying “Measuring…”?

The app needs battery-percentage changes before it can calculate a rate. Keep it open and check that the phone is charging. Slow charging and a nearly full battery can make this take longer. A “Likely Slow” message is an early indication, not a precise power measurement.

Why is charging slow or paused?

Try comparing another cable, charger or power source under similar conditions. At 80% and above, charging can slow substantially. A full battery, a charge limit or Optimised Battery Charging can also pause charging. These results do not by themselves prove a charger or cable is faulty.

What if battery information is unavailable?

Check that you are using the app on a physical iPhone and reopen it. If the message persists, contact support with your iPhone model, iOS version and app version.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Charging measurements and saved setups work on your device without an internet connection. Sending a support email or viewing these web pages requires connectivity.

How do I delete my saved setups?

Open Setups and tap Delete beside each entry you want to remove. Deleting the app from your device also removes its local data; offloading the app may retain it. Device backups may contain earlier copies, depending on your backup settings.

Contact support

Email us with the subject Charge Check support. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, what happened and the steps to reproduce it. For charging questions, describe the charger, cable and starting battery percentage. Screenshots are optional; remove any personal information you do not want to share.
