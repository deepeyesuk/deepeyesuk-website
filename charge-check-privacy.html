Privacy Policy

How Charge Check handles battery information, saved setups and support enquiries.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

DEEPEYES UK provides Charge Check. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and contact us for support. For personal information you send us, DEEPEYES UK is the data controller. Contact: support@deepeyes.co.uk.

2. Information processed on your device

Charge Check reads battery level, charging state, Low Power Mode status and your device model identifier to estimate charging performance. A model identifier describes the device model; it is not a unique device identifier. Measurements and calculations are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

If you save a setup, the app stores its label, estimated wattage, device model identifier, a locally generated record ID, and creation and update timestamps in local app storage. Live measurements are held in memory during use; the app does not upload a charging history.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting integrations. We do not receive your saved setups and do not sell app data.

3. Storage and deletion

Saved setups remain on your device until you delete them. Open Setups and tap Delete to remove an entry. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading may keep that data. Your operating system or device backup service may retain copies according to your settings. We cannot access or delete those backups.

4. If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if included, and the message, attachments and technical details you choose to provide. We use these to respond, investigate problems and maintain relevant support records. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing support and improving the reliability of the app.

Correspondence is processed through the email services used to send and receive it. We retain it only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry, handle related follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the nature of the issue and whether a dispute or legal requirement remains outstanding. Please avoid sending passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

5. Platform services and these web pages

Apple handles App Store distribution and may process information under its own privacy policy and your device settings. Device backups and any diagnostics you share through operating-system settings are separate from Charge Check’s local processing. See Apple’s privacy policy.

When you visit this support website, its hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to serve and secure the pages. These Charge Check pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies or externally hosted fonts.

6. Your privacy rights

For personal information we hold, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, and portability where applicable. You may object to our processing based on legitimate interests. These rights depend on the circumstances. Send requests to support@deepeyes.co.uk. We cannot retrieve measurements or setups held only on your device.

You can also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

7. Children’s information

The app does not ask users of any age to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, contact us so we can address it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or our information practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Any new data practices will be described in an updated policy.
