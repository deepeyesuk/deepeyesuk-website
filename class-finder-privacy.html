Class Finder for MoreYoga

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DEEPEYES UK ("we", "us", or "our") handles your data when you use the Class Finder for MoreYoga iOS application (the "App"). In short: we do not collect any personal data.

Class Finder is an independent, unofficial app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by MoreYoga.

1. Data We Collect

None. The App has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind, and we do not receive any information about you or how you use the App.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The studios you star and your studio filter choices are saved on your device so the App remembers them between sessions. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services

To show class timetables, the App requests the publicly available timetable directly from moreyoga.co.uk. As with any web request, MoreYoga's servers can see your device's IP address and which studios and week were requested. We have no access to this information. MoreYoga's handling of it is covered by their own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because we do not hold any.

4. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You can remove everything the App has saved at any time by deleting the App. If you have questions about your rights under UK GDPR, contact us at support@deepeyes.co.uk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a future version of the App begins to collect any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@deepeyes.co.uk
  • Subject line: "Privacy — Class Finder"
